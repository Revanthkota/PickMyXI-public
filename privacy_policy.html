Privacy Policy for PickMy XI

Last Updated: January 14, 2025

PickMy XI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Important: By using PickMy XI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Display Name: Your chosen captain name for match creation
  • Team Names: Names you create for teams in matches
  • Player Names: Names of players you add to matches
  • Match Data: Draft selections, toss results, and match outcomes

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features accessed, time spent in app, interaction patterns
  • Network Information: Internet connection status and type
  • Advertising ID: For personalized ad delivery (can be reset in device settings)

1.3 Anonymous Authentication

We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to provide you with app functionality without requiring personal registration. An anonymous user ID is automatically generated for your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Core Functionality: Creating matches, managing drafts, and storing match history
  • Match Coordination: Sharing match codes between captains for real-time collaboration
  • Data Synchronization: Real-time updates across devices during drafts
  • App Improvement: Understanding usage patterns to enhance features
  • Personalized Advertising: Delivering relevant ads through Google AdMob
  • Version Management: Notifying users of app updates via Firebase Remote Config

3. Third-Party Services

PickMy XI uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

3.1 Firebase (Google)

  • Firebase Authentication: Anonymous user authentication
  • Cloud Firestore: Storing match data, player information, and draft selections
  • Firebase Remote Config: Managing app versions and feature releases
  • Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3.2 Google AdMob

  • Purpose: Displaying advertisements within the app
  • Data Collected: Advertising ID, device information, approximate location, ad interaction data
  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Ad Personalization: Manage ad settings

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Data Storage

  • All match data is stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore
  • Data is stored in Google Cloud's data centers with industry-standard security
  • Active matches are available for real-time collaboration
  • Match history is retained for your reference

4.2 Data Security

We implement security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Firestore security rules to protect your data
  • Access controls limiting data visibility to match participants
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
Note: No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 With Other Users

  • Match codes and player names are shared between captains participating in the same match
  • Draft selections and results are visible to both captains in a match
  • No personal contact information is shared between users

5.2 With Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Firebase and AdMob as described above
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale

6. Your Data Rights

6.1 Access and Control

  • View Data: Access your match history and player data within the app
  • Delete Matches: Currently, matches remain in history; future updates may add deletion
  • Reset Advertising ID: Disable ad personalization in device settings

6.2 Account Deletion

Since we use anonymous authentication, uninstalling the app removes your local data. To request complete data deletion from our servers, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

PickMy XI is suitable for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside your country of residence. By using the app, you consent to such transfers. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information.

9. Advertising and Analytics

9.1 AdMob Advertising

  • We display ads through Google AdMob
  • Ads are shown at strategic points (after match completion, navigation transitions)
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings

9.2 Opting Out

Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

10. Data Retention

  • Match Data: Retained indefinitely for your match history
  • Anonymous Auth: Retained while you use the app
  • Usage Data: Aggregated analytics retained for app improvement

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via Firebase Remote Config.

Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
